description: |-
  Lament for his death son, asking the Gods the reason for such a punishment. You have come, young man, most excellent of the caretakers,
  That you may continue our unfortunate journey.
  You have been saved by the arrow that was aimed at me:
  Not yours, but mine, has thus enviously snatched it away.
  I was now destroyed by death, by my country's hatreds; of mine:
  My own son receives you in this light.
  The gods erred: why is it strange that they erred?
  Thus perished the two young men without a source.
  As much light now as there is, as much life now as there is,
  I owe to both, even though it may be unequal love.
  Do you ask, whence this wrath of the gods arose for me?
  The heavens had believed their own fates.

text: |-
  Venisti iuvenis, duclissima curatuorum,
  Infoelix nostrum ut prosequereris iter.
  Excepta est a te, quae in me directa sagitta:
  Non tua, sed mea sors, inuida sic rapuit.
  Delebar morti, patriae iam odijsque; meorum:
  Suscipit hoc te lum filius ipse meum.
  Errarunt superi: quid mirum errasse sentum?
  Infontes iuvenes sic periere duo.
  Quantum nunc lucis, quantum est nunc vita superstes,
  Debebo utrisque et sit licet impar amor.
  Quaeritis, unde mihi nata est haec ira deorum?
  Crediderant coeli fata reclusa sui.
